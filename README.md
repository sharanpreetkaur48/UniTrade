# ShareShelf

> **What if a campus wasted less, created more, and grew together?**  
> **Reuse. Make. Share.**

ShareShelf is a college-first marketplace concept designed to connect students who have something to offer with students who need it. It brings second-hand goods, student-made products, and student skills into one campus-focused space.

## The Idea

Campus exchanges often happen through scattered WhatsApp groups, personal contacts, and social media posts. Requests can get buried, useful items may go unused, and students who make products or offer services can struggle to reach potential buyers.

ShareShelf aims to make these exchanges easier to discover and organise in one place, starting with a single college.

## What Students Can Exchange

### ♻️ Reuse

Find or list pre-owned items such as:

- Textbooks and academic materials
- Calculators and drawing instruments
- Hostel and other permitted student essentials

### 🎨 Make

Help student creativity find an audience through:

- Handmade crafts and crochet
- Art, personalised gifts, and small creative products
- Other student-made items

### 🤝 Share

Help students discover skills and services offered by their peers, such as:

- Graphic design and presentation design
- Video editing and photography
- Web development and tutoring

The initial category mix and launch order should be validated with students rather than assumed to have equal demand.

## Key Feature: Wanted Board

The **Wanted Board** lets a student post an item or service they are looking for. Other students can browse requests and respond if they can help.

This creates two-way discovery:

- **Offer:** List something you have made, own, or can do.
- **Wanted:** Post something you need and connect with a relevant student.

## Intended User Journey

1. A student creates an account and selects their college.
2. They browse or search available listings, or check the Wanted Board.
3. They contact an interested student to ask questions and agree on terms.
4. The buyer and seller or service provider arrange payment and handover directly.
5. Users can report suspicious listings or concerns for review, where moderation is implemented.

## Trust and Safety

ShareShelf is intended to support a more organised campus exchange—not to promise a risk-free marketplace.

Planned safeguards may include:

- Clear item-condition descriptions and real-item photos for pre-owned goods.
- Clear labels distinguishing finished handmade products from made-to-order examples.
- Service descriptions that explain scope, price, and expected deliverables.
- Listing and user reporting with proportionate moderation.
- Privacy-conscious student eligibility checks, subject to a feasible and appropriate process.

Student verification, if implemented, would only indicate that an eligibility check was completed; it would not guarantee a user's honesty. Verification, moderation, payment handling, and dispute procedures must be designed and tested before being presented as operational features.

## Scope and Rollout

**Initial focus:** One college, with campus-based discovery and handover.

The proposed path is:

1. Define and build the core marketplace experience.
2. Learn whether students want to list, search, and exchange across the proposed categories.
3. Seek any required institutional permission and test a small campus pilot.
4. Measure actual usage and completed exchanges before considering expansion.

Expansion to other campuses, managed delivery, protected payments, and advanced recommendation or fraud-detection features are not assumed to be part of the initial version.

## Measuring Success

Rather than relying only on sign-ups or downloads, a pilot should track:

- Genuine, active listings by category
- Student enquiries and unsuccessful searches
- Enquiry-to-completed-exchange rate, based on user confirmation
- Repeat use by buyers and sellers or service providers
- Reports, disputes, and how promptly they are reviewed
- Whether students return to use the marketplace again

These are proposed measures, not results or claims of current traction.

## Current Status

ShareShelf is an early-stage project concept being developed for a student startup and hackathon pitch. The product is incomplete, and user research and marketplace validation have not yet been established.

The next priority is to build and test the core experience, speak with students, and assess whether a one-college pilot is feasible.

No college partnership, institutional approval, active-user count, completed transaction count, or revenue is claimed here.
